What each role can do
  • user: contributes records and proposals, votes, keeps lists.
  • curator: also reviews and approves other people's proposals and can import from providers.
  • admin: also manages this node: settings, peers, tokens, backups and these users.
  • suspended: cannot sign in; open sessions and API tokens end immediately. Their contributions stay.
  • external sign-in: may also sign in through a trusted sign-in provider (Sign-in sharing).
  • sees data from your nodes: sees what this node received from your other nodes, with where it came from, even while visitors do not (Sharing). Administrators and curators always do, and so does every account while registration is closed; an account that registered itself does not unless you tick this.
