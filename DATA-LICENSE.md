# Data license

Metadata contributed by users and curators to an Atlas authority is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

This does not cover data fetched from third-party providers (Steam, IGDB, RAWG, etc.). That data remains subject to each provider's terms, is cached by the node that fetched it, and is not redistributed through the federation feed unless the provider's terms allow it.

## Contract files

The machine-readable contract files in `contracts/` (the shared vocabulary, `openapi-client-v1.json` and the
generated, synthetic `client-fixtures/`) and the contract fixtures under `tests/fixtures/` whose names contain
`contract` (such as `resolve-contract-v1.json`) are also offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/),
so that clients under any licence (for example the MIT-licensed Atlas connectors) can copy and pin them. Contributions
to those files are accepted under the same dedication.
